Privacy Policy

Khmer Calendar · KM Soft · last updated 18 August 2026

Khmer Calendar does not collect your personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, and no analytics or advertising of any kind. We operate no server that receives your data.

What is stored, and where

Everything the app remembers is stored on your own device, in your browser or Office task pane's local storage. None of it is transmitted to us or to anyone else.

WhatStored as
Language, theme, first day of weekkh-cal-lang, kh-cal-theme, kh-cal-startday
The place name used in dateskh-cal-place
Your chosen weather location, and a short-lived copy of the last forecastkh-cal-weather-location, kh-cal-weather-cache
Health tracking entries, if you use that featurekh-cal-health-profiles, kh-cal-health-profile-*

Health tracking

The app includes an optional menstrual cycle tracker. Any dates you record are held only on your device and are never uploaded, shared or transmitted anywhere. We cannot see them, and neither can anyone else unless they have access to your device.

Clearing your browser data, or removing the app, deletes these entries permanently. There is no backup and no way for us to recover them.

The one external request

If you open the weather feature, the app sends the coordinates of the location you chose to Open-Meteo in order to retrieve a forecast. That request carries no name, no account and no identifier — only a latitude and longitude. If you never open the weather feature, no external request is made at all.

Open-Meteo is an independent service with its own privacy policy. If you would rather not use it, simply do not open the weather tab; every other part of the app works offline.

Location

If you ask the app to use your current location for weather, your device will prompt you for permission first. The coordinates are used for that forecast and kept on your device. You can decline, and instead type a city name.

In Word, Excel and PowerPoint

The add-in requests the ReadWriteDocument permission. This is used for one purpose: to write a date or a number in words into the document at your request, when you click a button. The add-in does not read your document, does not send its contents anywhere, and does not retain anything from it.

Children

The app is a calendar and collects nothing, so it is suitable for all ages.
